Numberwake: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Numberwake is a math game for young children. We built it so that it does not collect personal information. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not do with data.

The short version

  • We do not collect, store or share any personal information. Children's names never leave the device.
  • No accounts, no email, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The app works without an internet connection.
  • If a grown-up turns on the optional family backup, each child's grade and progress are kept online under a private family code, without names.
  • If the app runs into an error, it sends us a short technical crash report (no names, no progress, nothing that identifies anyone).

What stays on your device

To work, the app saves the following on the phone or tablet it is installed on:

  • each child's progress (skills practiced and mastered, stars, minutes played per day);
  • children's names or nicknames, if a grown-up types them in (optional; the app also works with "Player 1"). Names are only ever kept on the device;
  • the grown-up's settings (daily play time, breaks, quiet hours, music on or off);
  • whether the full version is open (a Family plan subscription or a one-time purchase).

A grown-up can erase a child's progress at any time in the Grown-ups area. Uninstalling the app deletes everything on the device.

Family backup (optional, off until a grown-up turns it on)

In the Grown-ups area (behind a parent gate) a grown-up can turn on a family backup, so progress isn't lost when a phone is replaced and can be shared with a second device. When it is on, the app keeps a copy of the following online, under a random 12-character family code made on the device:

  • for each child: their place in the list ("child 1", "child 2"), grade, state standards choice, picture choice, and progress (skills, answers right or wrong with their times, stars, minutes played per day);
  • the play-time settings.

The backup never contains names, email addresses, contact details, location, or anything else that identifies a child or a family. Anyone who has the family code can see and change that family's backup, so keep the code private.

The backup is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, United States), which acts as our service provider. To use it, the device signs in anonymously: Firebase gives the device a random ID, with no name or email. A grown-up can stop the backup on a device, or delete the family's backup for every device, at any time in Grown-ups → Account → Family backup. Deleting removes it from our service right away.

Crash reports

So that we can find and fix problems, when the app or the game runs into an error the app sends a crash report to the same Firebase service. A report contains only: the error's technical message and the place in our code where it happened, the app version, the device model (for example "samsung SM-A135F") and system version (for example "android 13"), and the time. It contains no names, no progress, no family code and no device or advertising ID. Reports are read only by us, are used only to fix the app, and are kept no longer than 90 days. At most 10 reports a day are sent from a device.

Purchases

The optional Family plan (a monthly or yearly subscription) is bought through Google Play (or the Apple App Store). The store handles payment, renewals, cancellations and account details under its own privacy policy; we never see payment or account information. The app only asks the store whether a plan or purchase is active, so it can open the full game, restore it on another device, and close it again if a subscription ends. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in the store account.

Plans and purchases are only offered in the Grown-ups area, behind a parent gate. See the terms of use for how the Family plan works.

Internet and permissions

The game runs from a small server inside the app on the device itself. The app uses the internet only to buy, restore or check a plan or purchase with the store, for the family backup when a grown-up has turned it on, and to send crash reports. Like any internet service, the servers it connects to (Google Play, the App Store, Firebase) see the device's IP address while connecting; we do not keep it. The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Children's privacy

Numberwake is designed for children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13: children's names stay on the device, the optional backup holds only progress under a random code, and crash reports hold only technical details used to keep the app working. We never sell or share any of it, and we never use it for advertising. This is consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and with Google Play's Families policy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. We will not start collecting personal information without updating this policy first.
