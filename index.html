Bolt Math: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Bolt Math is a math game for young children. We built it so that it does not collect personal information. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not do with data.

The short version

  • We do not collect, store or share any personal information.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Everything the app remembers stays on your device.
  • The app works without an internet connection.

What stays on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on the phone or tablet it is installed on. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • each child's progress (skills practised and mastered, stars, minutes played per day);
  • children's names or nicknames, if a grown-up types them in (optional; the app also works with "Player 1");
  • the grown-up's settings (daily play time, breaks, quiet hours, music on or off);
  • whether the full version is open (a Family plan subscription or a one-time purchase).

A grown-up can erase a child's progress at any time in the Grown-ups area. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

The optional Family plan (a monthly or yearly subscription) and the one-time "Grade 1 forever" purchase are bought through Google Play (or the Apple App Store). The store handles payment, renewals, cancellations and account details under its own privacy policy; we never see payment or account information. The app only asks the store whether a plan or purchase is active, so it can open the full game, restore it on another device, and close it again if a subscription ends. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in the store account.

Plans and purchases are only offered in the Grown-ups area, behind a parent gate. See the terms of use for how the Family plan works.

Internet and permissions

The game runs from a small server inside the app on the device itself. The app uses the internet only to buy, restore or check a plan or purchase with the store. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Children's privacy

Bolt Math is designed for children. Because it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, there is nothing for us to access, share or sell. This is consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and with Google Play's Families policy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. We will not start collecting personal information without updating this policy first.
