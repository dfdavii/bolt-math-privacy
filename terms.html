Free to start

The first units of the app are free. The rest is opened by the Family plan or by a one-time "Grade 1 forever" purchase.

Family plan (subscription)

  • The Family plan opens every grade available in the app, new adventures as they are added, and the full parent report, for up to 4 children on the device.
  • It is offered monthly ($4.99) or yearly ($39.99); prices can differ by country and are shown in the app before you buy.

  • You can manage or cancel the plan at any time in your Google Play or App Store account settings. Cancelling stops future renewals; the plan stays active until the end of the period already paid.

Grade 1 forever

A single payment ($9.99) that opens all of Grade 1 on your account, with no subscription. It does not include other grades.
